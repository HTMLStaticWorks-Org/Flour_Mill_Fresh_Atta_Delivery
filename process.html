Quality & Temperature Blueprint

The 4-Stage Sub-38°C Sanitary Milling Journey

High-speed industrial roller mills generate frictional heat up to 88°C, scorching living amylase enzymes and volatilizing natural germ oils. Here is how our slow granite milling maintains pure nutrient integrity.

01

Triple Air-Cyclone De-Stoning

Multi-stage aspiration and vibratory sieves remove chaff, dust particles, and field pebbles without water soaking, keeping raw grain kernel structures dry and unfermented.

Purity Benchmark 99.98% Clean Grains
02

Solar Moisture Equilibrium

Grains rest in food-grade 304 stainless steel aerated hoppers calibrated between 10.5% and 11.2% relative moisture, ensuring optimal bran cleavage during grinding without mold risk.

Target Moisture 10.8% Equilibrium
03

Natural Granite Slow Friction

Hand-dressed natural granite millstones turn at an ultra-low 280 RPM. The thick stone thermal mass absorbs kinetic friction, strictly keeping grinding heat below 35°C.

Stone Temp Cap < 35°C Sub-Cold
04

Hermetic Kraft Sack & Stamp

Zero plastics or synthetic liners. Atta drops directly into breathable 3-ply unbleached virgin kraft paper sacks, stitched shut and stamped with the exact hour and date of milling.

Dispatch Window Under 4 - 6 Hours

Our Zero-Compromise Freshness Guarantee

No chemical bleaching (Benzoyl Peroxide), no potassium bromate, and no months of warehouse storage.
